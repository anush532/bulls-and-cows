BULLS AND COWS

Guess the secret 4-digit number. No repeats, and it never starts with 0.

Main Menu

How to Play

  • The computer generates a secret 4-digit number.
  • All digits are different, and the first digit is not 0.
  • Bull: correct digit in the correct position.
  • Cow: correct digit in the wrong position.
  • Win by getting 4 Bulls.

Example: Secret = 4271, Guess = 4729 → 1 Bull, 2 Cows.
